About us

A clearer view of cricket.

CricketVX is a cricket statistics website built around one idea: finding reliable information about a player should not require digging through half a dozen sites.

Our mission

Cricket generates an enormous amount of data — every innings, every over, every delivery. Most of it is scattered across scorecards, PDFs, and sites that were never designed to be searched.

We are building CricketVX to be the opposite: a single, fast, readable place to look up a cricketer and understand who they are and how they have performed. No clutter, no pop-ups, no guessing which number is current.

CricketVX began as a small project built by people who follow cricket closely and got tired of piecing statistics together by hand.

What we're building

We ship in stages. Everything below is on the roadmap; the status tells you honestly where each piece stands today.

  • Live Player search by name
  • Live Player profiles — team, role, batting and bowling style, format stats
  • In progress Match pages with innings summaries and scorecards
  • In progress Team pages and squad rosters
  • In progress Series and tournament pages

What we care about

These are the principles we use when deciding what to build and what to leave out.

✓ Accuracy over volume

A smaller set of correct statistics is worth more than a large set nobody trusts. We would rather show nothing than show a number we cannot stand behind.

◎ Clear attribution

Our data comes from a named provider and we say so on every page. We do not present someone else's work as our own.

⚡ Speed and simplicity

Plain pages that load quickly, work on a phone, and do not require an account to read a batting average.

↻ Honest status

If a feature is not ready, we label it as in progress instead of showing placeholder data that looks real.

Where our data comes from

CricketVX does not maintain its own database. All cricket statistics on this site are retrieved from a specialist data provider.

Powered by Avcros Datalink

Every player profile, statistic, and — as they become available — match and team record on CricketVX is served by the Avcros Datalink API. Avcros collects and verifies the underlying cricket data; our job is to present it clearly.

This means we do not store, resell, or alter the statistics. If you believe a figure on this site is wrong, it most likely originates upstream, and we will raise it with Avcros.
